About Us

Hi! We are Klaudia and Szymon, the creative duo behind @para_lata & @stayditorial. Specializing in luxury slow travel and editorial storytelling, we translate the soul of a destination into a visual narrative.

From the historic serenity of Sanctuary Mandela to the refined landscapes of La Petite Ferme, our work focuses on the quiet, sophisticated moments that define a guest’s experience.

Based in London and working worldwide, we partner with heritage brands and boutique hotels to craft timeless content that resonates with the modern, conscious traveler.
